## 2. Text

```
To 민수 ♡

Okay. I'm not lost anymore. I'm only a LITTLE lost.
I found 머루 and 미나리!! I ate the 머루 and almost cried,
it was so good. I saved you some. Not a lot. Sorry ♡

A hiking man with like a hundred pockets told me
real wild 노루 live up top. Rare ones!!
He also told me where he pooped. So. 50% trust.

Go up the hill to the big dead 나무.
The white twisty one, like a skeleton doing yoga.
Walk right through it and keep climbing.
I'll wait for you up there~ probably!!
(Do 노루 eat people?? Don't answer that.)

Some plants here won't tell you their name.
I picked one. My face is fine now. Mostly.

— 수민
p.s. you still owe me 고기 from MT
```

Line breaks are for reading here. The notebook layout may rewrap them.

## 5. Seeds and cameos

- **"Do 노루 eat people??"** foreshadows the white deer.
- **"Won't tell you their name" / "My face is fine now. Mostly."** nods to the "???" decoys and the 큰천남성 face swell, without saying whether decoys help or harm.
- **The hundred-pocket hiker who reports his poop** is an unnamed cameo by **동철수** 🟡 (kept for now).
- **"고기 from MT"** sets up the MT trip that 수민 and 민수 got lost on.
