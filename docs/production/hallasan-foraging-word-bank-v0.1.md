## 2. The map ✅

- The foraging map **runs uphill**, from lower forest to **alpine grassland**.
- **구상나무 (Korean fir)** appears as **scenery** only, in the upper zone. Nothing to forage on it.

### Zones 🟡

| Zone | Plants placed there |
|---|---|
| Lower forest | 마, 머루, 미나리 (near water), 고사리, 고비, 큰천남성, 붓순나무 |
| Mid slope | 노루귀 |
| Alpine grassland | 시로미, 들쭉나무; 구상나무 groves as scenery |

Zone placement is a suggestion for the level layout; 시로미, 들쭉나무 and 구상나무 are alpine plants (see §6).

## 4. Decoy plants ("???" on hover)

### 4.1 Cost a heart

| Korean | What it is | Gore 🟡 |
|---|---|---|
| **큰천남성** | cobra lily (poisonous) | Face swells, then pops |
| **붓순나무** | Japanese star anise (toxic fruit, looks like edible star anise) | Convulsions; eyes pop out |

### 4.2 Give a reward

| Korean | What it is | Reward 🟡 |
|---|---|---|
| **노루귀** | hepatica (spring flower) | Slow-time charm |
| **들쭉나무** | bog bilberry | Spelling hint (greyed-out letter flickers on the right spot for 2 seconds) |

Decoy names don't need to be readable: players only ever see "???". Their names can appear in the 도감 later.
