## 1. Why these exist ✅

Most need words in the animal scene (나무, 고기, 거미, 고구마, 오이, 우리) were never taught in Korean. The entry gives the English and the thought bubble gives a picture, so the player knows **what** the animal wants but not **how to write it**. A hint letter is a scrap of found writing in full Korean where the target word is the shape that repeats. A beginner can't read it, but can spot the repeating block and copy it.

## 2. Rules ✅

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Trigger:** 120 s with no progress, then a letter floats in (exit-note style) for the **oldest unfinished word need** |
| 2 | **Read once, then thrown away.** Not collectible, not kept in the backpack |
| 3 | **One letter per need word.** Item needs (머루, 미나리) get none: the item is already in the backpack |
| 4 | **The target word repeats more than any other word** (4–7 times) |
| 5 | **Found writing, not letters from animals.** Memos, lists, calendar pages, flyers, ads, package scraps, itineraries, receipts, notes. The author is usually an irrelevant mystery. The 노루's 우리 letter is the one exception: animals that write or speak stay rare |
| 6 | **Vibe:** satirical, sarcastic, humorous, absurd, creepy, eerie, original. General direction, not rules. Each letter has its own flavor |
| 7 | **English glosses are for the team only.** They are never shown in the game |
| 8 | "Progress" for the trigger is defined in the DEV prompt 🟡 |

## 3. The letters ✅

### 3.1 우리 (pen) — letter from a 노루 · ×6

```
우리는 우리 안에 있어요.
엄마는 우리 밖으로 나갔어요.
엄마는 돌아오지 않았어요.
사람들이 "노루가 너무 많아"라고 했어요.
우리 안은 안전해요.
우리 안은 안전해요.
우리 안은 안전해요.

— 7,033번째 노루
```
*We are inside the pen. / Mom went outside the pen. / Mom didn't come back. / People said, "There are too many roe deer." / Inside the pen is safe. ×3 / — Roe deer no. 7,033*

Note: uses both meanings of 우리 (pen; we). The signature follows the 7,032 노루 culled as pests in 2013–2019 (word bank §9).

### 3.2 나무 (tree) — work to-do list · ×6

```
한라산 나무 관리팀
오늘 할 일 (10월 14일)
1. 나무 세기
2. 나무 다시 세기
3. 나무 한 그루 없어짐. 보고서 쓰기
4. 없어진 나무 찾기
5. 나무 뒤에 서 있는 사람은 세지 않기
```
*Hallasan Tree Management Team. To do today (Oct 14): 1. Count trees. 2. Count trees again. 3. One tree missing. Write a report. 4. Find the missing tree. 5. Don't count the person standing behind the tree.*

### 3.3 고기 (meat) — flyer · ×6

### 3.4 거미 (spider) — torn piece of a product box · ×5

```
거미 퇴치기 3000
거미가 사라져요!
사용법: 거미에게 뿌리세요.

```
*Spider Repeller 3000. Spiders disappear! How to use: spray it at the spider. Caution: the spider may get angry. Caution: big spiders cannot be returned.*

### 3.5 노루 (roe deer) — package tour itinerary · ×5

```
제주 가을 패키지 2박 3일
1일차: 공항 → 노루생태관찰원
2일차: 한라산 등반, 노루 사진 찍기
3일차: 노루 육포 공장 견학 → 면세점
※ 노루를 만지지 마세요.
※ 노루가 만지면 신고하세요.
```
*Jeju Autumn Package, 3 days 2 nights. Day 1: Airport → Roe Deer Ecology Park. Day 2: Climb Hallasan, take roe deer photos. Day 3: Roe deer jerky factory tour → duty-free shop. ※ Don't touch the roe deer. ※ If a roe deer touches you, report it.*

Note: the jerky factory is fictional and points at the cull, like 3.1.

### 3.6 고구마 (sweet potato) — calendar page · ×5

```
10월
7일  고구마 캐기
8일  고구마 세기. 하나 없음.
9일  고구마 또 없어짐.
12일 밭에 발자국. 노루?
15일 고구마 지키기. 밤새.
16일 고구마가 나를 보고 있다.
```
*October. 7th: dig up sweet potatoes. 8th: count sweet potatoes. One missing. 9th: another sweet potato gone. 12th: footprints in the field. Roe deer? 15th: guard the sweet potatoes. All night. 16th: The sweet potato is watching me.*

```
찾습니다
고라니

마지막으로 본 날: 아주 오래전

```
*MISSING: water deer. Last seen: Hallasan. Last seen: a very long time ago. Features: has fangs. Screams loudly. Reward: one water deer. If you see the water deer, contact us. If the water deer sees you, run.*

### 3.8 오리 (duck) — receipt · ×5

```
영 수 증
백록담 오리배 대여 (1시간)   15,000원
오리 먹이 2봉지               4,000원
오리 사진 촬영                3,000원
오리 사과문                       0원
합계                         22,000원
오리에게 사과하셨습니까? □ 예 □ 아니요
```
*RECEIPT. Baengnokdam duck boat rental (1 hr) ₩15,000. Duck food, 2 bags ₩4,000. Duck photo shoot ₩3,000. Apology letter to the duck ₩0. Total ₩22,000. Did you apologize to the duck? □ Yes □ No*

Note: the receipt's columns must line up in the game font. Use a monospace layout or right-aligned prices.

### 3.9 오이 (cucumber) — passive-aggressive note on a shelter fridge · ×7

```
냉장고 안 오이 먹은 사람 보세요.
제 오이입니다.
오이는 세 개였어요.
지금 오이는 한 개예요.
오이에 이름도 썼어요.
오이를 돌려주세요.
오이는 알고 있어요.
```
*To whoever ate the cucumber in the fridge. It's my cucumber. There were three cucumbers. Now there is one. I even wrote my name on the cucumber. Give the cucumber back. The cucumber knows.*

## 5. Checks

- 🔍 **Native-speaker review** of all nine, especially 거미 퇴치기 (3.4) and the 오리배 joke (3.8).
- 🔍 **노루생태관찰원** is a real Jeju park (not a brand). Confirm the name before release.
- ✅ **노루 eat 고구마** (word bank §9, Seoul Shinmun 2012). October is harvest time 🔍.
- ✅ **고라니:** fangs, a loud scream, extinct on Hallasan (word bank §9).
- ✅ **7,032 노루 culled, 2013–2019** (word bank §9, Seoul Shinmun 2019).
- ✅ **No real people or brands.** The restaurant, the repeller, the jerky factory and the duck-boat rental are fictional.
- 🟡 Boating on 백록담 is obviously not allowed. That's the joke.
