## 1. Rules this word bank follows

| # | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | *Hallasan* letters: **ㅏ ㅓ ㅗ ㅜ ㅣ · ㄱ ㄴ ㄹ ㅁ · silent ㅇ** (10). **Silent ㅇ** is this scene's new letter | Log B §3; Log G |
| 2 | Animals must live, or have lived, on Hallasan. **Extinct animals are prioritized** and appear **alive, unexplained** | Log G |
| 3 | Bonus words have **at most 2 unknowns**; a 받침 counts as one. **Exception: 도마뱀** (4) | Word bank v0.1 §1 #3; Log G |
| 4 | Words are built **syllable by syllable** | Log B §4.1 |
| 5 | Needs are **discovered by watching** an animal; an entry with empty blocks appears | Log G |
| 6 | A need is met by **spelling a word** or **giving a backpack item** the animal really eats. **One animal can be another's need** | Log G |
| 7 | **Gore: predators hunt other animals on screen**, over the top. A hunted animal's letter is lost. *Replaces* the old "never to animals" rule (Log A §3.7 #3, Roadmap §0 #15, Word bank v0.1 §1 #7) | Log G |
| 8 | Tools never hurt animals 🟡 | Log G |
| 9 | Season: **October** | Log G |

## 6. Danger ✅

### 6.1 Damage per hit (rolls 1 to max, in half hearts)

| Animal | Max | 1/4 death roll |
|---|---|---|
| 오리, 족제비, 비바리뱀 | ½ ♥ | — |
| 노루, 고라니, 오소리 | 1 ♥ | — |
| 사슴, 큰부리까마귀 | 1½ ♥ | — |
| 검독수리 | 2 ♥ | ✅ |
| 들개 | 2 ♥ | ✅ |
| 멧돼지 | 2½ ♥ | ✅ |
| Giant bear | Kills | — |

- **Bare hands:** ½ ♥ for less dangerous animals, 1 ♥ for more dangerous ones. For predators, there's a 1/4 chance of death, otherwise the table above applies.
- **Predator attacks:** about once every 3 minutes, with the same 1/4 death roll.
- **A death kills both travelers** (as in foraging).

### 6.2 The timer and the bear ✅

- The timer is random, **6–12 min**. ⏰ Revisit after playtests.
- When the timer ends and the words aren't done, the **giant brown bear** (빌레못동굴) kills both travelers.
- If the players are stuck early, they **wait** for more animals.
- When every word is done, the **exit note** floats in and points to the exit.
- The white deer's boss word stays.
